How the wallet works
1 · You pay for the plan. The discounted amount lands in your SpoonUp wallet as a plan balance, ring-fenced to that subscription.

2 · We deduct per delivery. Each jar delivered draws its own rate — you always see the remaining balance and remaining deliveries.
3 · Skip freely. Skipped days move to the end of the plan, so nothing is lost.

4 · No withdrawals. Plan balance isn't cashable. Genuine exceptions are handled over email, case by case.
